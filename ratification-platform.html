Staged approval pipeline on PostgreSQL

Forensic record
PostgreSQL, web appIn production

Replacing a 44-step manual certification process, spread across six systems and tracked by hand, with a pipeline that holds its own state and applies a fixed decision rule.

Project scope

Certifying an open water swim record ran through a shared inbox. I mapped the process as it actually existed before proposing anything: 44 steps across six systems, with a tracker updated by hand at every stage that nothing wrote to automatically.

The audit surfaced what the process could not tell you. Three branches ended the workflow with no route back in. One form required a single field, and it was not the evidence. Nothing advanced unless a person noticed it should, which meant applicants who had paid and finished their swim could sit indefinitely with no status to give them.

Constraints

  • Live process with paying applicants throughout. No stop-the-world cutover.
  • Decisions are contested publicly, so every determination has to be reconstructable.
  • A volunteer review committee, so the system has to work for people who use it a few times a year.

Approach

  1. Map the existing process completely before designing anything, including the parts nobody would have mentioned.
  2. Give the pipeline its own state, so progress is a property of the record rather than of someone remembering.
  3. Fix the decision rule in the system instead of leaving it to whoever responds first.
  4. Hold the logic in the database rather than the application, so it cannot be bypassed by a different client.

Implementation

State in the record

Intake, evidence collection, review, determination and publication are explicit states on the application rather than stages in someone's head. An application always has a status, which means an applicant can always be told where they are.

Logic in database functions

The decision rule lives in Postgres functions rather than in the front end. A determination is produced the same way regardless of what calls it, and the path from evidence to outcome is inspectable after the fact.

Committee review

Reviewers read the full application rather than a summary and submit their determination from it. Reviews are counted against a fixed rule instead of closing whenever enough replies arrive.

Result

  • Every application has a status that can be stated without asking anyone.
  • Determinations are reconstructable from the record, which matters when one is challenged.
  • The audit of the old process is itself a deliverable, and documented the failure points that justified the rebuild.
